VIRAT KOHLI: PROFILE AND CAREER STATISTICS

Access level of this document: Employee (readable by every signed-in user). Figures are approximate and were last checked against public sources in mid-2025; verify before quoting them.

BIOGRAPHY

Virat Kohli was born on 5 November 1988 in Delhi, India. He is a right-handed top-order batter and an occasional medium-pace bowler. He grew up in West Delhi and trained at the West Delhi Cricket Academy under coach Rajkumar Sharma. His father, Prem Kohli, a criminal lawyer, died in December 2006, and Kohli returned to the field the next day to play a Ranji Trophy match for Delhi.

Kohli captained India to victory in the 2008 Under-19 World Cup in Malaysia. He made his One Day International debut on 18 August 2008 against Sri Lanka at Dambulla, his Test debut on 20 June 2011 against the West Indies at Kingston, and his Twenty20 International debut on 12 June 2010 against Zimbabwe at Harare.

He married the actor Anushka Sharma in December 2017. They have a daughter, Vamika, born in January 2021, and a son, Akaay, born in February 2024.

CAPTAINCY AND TEAM HONOURS

Kohli captained India in all three formats. As Test captain he led India in 68 matches and won 40 of them, the most wins by any Indian Test captain. He was a member of India's 2011 ODI World Cup winning squad, the 2013 Champions Trophy winning squad, the 2024 T20 World Cup winning squad and the 2025 Champions Trophy winning squad.

TEST RECORD

Matches: 123. Runs: 9,230. Batting average: 46.85. Centuries: 30. Half-centuries: 31. Highest score: 254 not out, made against South Africa at Pune in 2019. Kohli scored seven Test double centuries, the most by an Indian batter. He announced his retirement from Test cricket on 12 May 2025.

ONE DAY INTERNATIONAL RECORD

Matches: about 302. Runs: about 14,181. Batting average: about 57.9. Centuries: 51, the most in men's ODI history. Highest score: 183 against Pakistan at Mirpur in the 2012 Asia Cup. Kohli was the fastest batter to reach 8,000, 9,000, 10,000, 11,000, 12,000, 13,000 and 14,000 ODI runs. In the 2023 World Cup he scored 765 runs, a tournament record, and was named Player of the Tournament. His 50th ODI century came in the 2023 World Cup semi-final against New Zealand at the Wankhede Stadium.

TWENTY20 INTERNATIONAL RECORD

Matches: about 125. Runs: about 4,188. Batting average: about 48.7. Highest score: 122 not out against Afghanistan in the 2022 Asia Cup, his only T20I century. Kohli retired from T20 Internationals on 29 June 2024 after being named Player of the Match in India's final win at the T20 World Cup.

INDIAN PREMIER LEAGUE

Kohli has played for Royal Challengers Bengaluru (Bangalore) since the first season in 2008, the longest one-team association in the league. He has scored more than 8,600 IPL runs, the most in the competition, including eight centuries. In 2016 he scored 973 runs in a single season, including four centuries, which remains a record. Royal Challengers won their first IPL title in 2025.

AWARDS

Arjuna Award (2013), Padma Shri (2017), Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna (2018). ICC ODI Player of the Year in 2012, 2017, 2018 and 2023. Winner of the Sir Garfield Sobers Trophy as ICC Cricketer of the Year in 2017 and 2018.
